HOW TO PLAY

  • Switch on audio, then press or touch the keyboard. You can also use Z S X D C V G B H N J M.
  • Select OSC, FILTER, ENV, LFO, FX, or ARP. The four sliders act like the FM-1's physical knobs.
  • Press PLAY / ARP to generate repeating notes; press DRONE to hold a chord.
  • Use Save locally to retain your patch in this browser. Export a JSON patch for backup.

HARDWARE ROADMAP

Wavefeld is a working Web Audio prototype, not the actual FM-1's firmware and not a cycle-accurate emulator. The next step is to port its wavetable engine, modulation and front-panel mappings to a GPL-compatible FM-1 platform such as Dinghy, measure CPU/memory use, and test on hardware. This project does not contain Waldorf code, patches, or wavetables.

Independent experiment; not affiliated with Waldorf, M-VAVE or 1010music. No firmware installer is offered.
